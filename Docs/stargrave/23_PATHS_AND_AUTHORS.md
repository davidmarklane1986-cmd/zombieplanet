# Paths & Grand Authors

## The Five Paths (No explicit selection)
1. **Listener** — memory, echoes, understanding
2. **Echoforger** — creation, rewriting, shaping
3. **Gravetaker** — endings, decay, final truth
4. **Witness** — observation, neutrality, history
5. **Bound One** — obligation, sacrifice, control

## Path Rules
- Paths are inferred by actions, not chosen in menus.
- Alignment can shift over time.
- Relics and vaults respond dynamically to alignment.

## Grand Authors (Start + End Pillar)
- Five nomad-like figures appear at the beginning.
- They silently walk away; the player may follow or ignore.
- They are the only NPCs the player cannot directly interact with.
- If followed, they can be observed for a time, then vanish off-screen.
- Their appearance subtly changes in real time based on player progress in their corresponding path (visual progression like *Journey*).

## Ending Rule
- Endgame is reaching a **Library** where the player receives their story.
- The Grand Authors reappear to present the completed book.
- Endings are open-ended / cliffhanger-style and support MMO continuation.
