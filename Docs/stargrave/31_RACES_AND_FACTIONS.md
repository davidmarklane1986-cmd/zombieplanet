# Races & Factions

## Races
- 12 lore races in the universe.
- 4–6 playable initially.
- Each race must be:
  - Balanced and viable
  - Distinct in philosophy and flavor
  - Integrated into relics/vaults/paths

Each race includes:
- Base discoverable story
- Good/Neutral/Bad outcome philosophies
- Relic set (12 powerful balanced relics tied to noble/heroic history)
- Vault styles and echo dialogues

## Factions
Factions are collective groups (not necessarily races).
They include:
- Pirates (including a pirate race: Strayhold Exiles) and pirate overlays applicable mid-story
- Religions / ideological groups
- Political and militaristic blocs
- Scholarly / archivist forces

Faction Rules
- Factions span multiple planets.
- Factions generate missions via PME.
- Factions react to relic use and discoveries.
- Faction power can rise/fall based on player and world activity.
