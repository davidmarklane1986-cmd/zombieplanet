# Settlements (Growth & Decay)

## Purpose
Settlements create a living world and reflect faction progress.

## Growth
Settlements can evolve:
- Camp → Outpost → Village → Town → City

Growth influenced by:
- Player presence and repeated visits
- Missions completed for local NPCs/factions
- Resource flow (deliveries, trade)
- Faction control and protection

## Decay
If abandoned:
- NPCs leave
- Structures become ruins
- Campfire/markers fade
- Site may sink/erode and eventually despawn (hours-scale, world-time dependent)

## Persistence Rule
- Settlement state is part of world state and must be saved.
- Settlement ruins can become exploration content (environmental storytelling).
