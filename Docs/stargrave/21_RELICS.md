# Relics

## Relic Philosophy (Core Pillar)
Relics are **narrative operators**, not generic loot.
They:
- Change how the world reacts to the player.
- Alter NPC behavior, perception, and events.
- Carry moral/philosophical weight tied to paths and races.

## Relic Inventory (Minimum Scope)
- ≥60 relics
- Structure: **12 races × 5 paths**
- Each relic must be powerful but balanced through tradeoffs, consequences, and situational use.

## Relic Types
1. **True Relic Sites**
   - Fixed, canonical world locations.
   - Discoverable through exploration and subtle environmental cues.
2. **Shadow Copies**
   - Non-canonical duplicates used for pacing or misdirection.
   - Provide partial/altered effects.
   - Do not reveal true site locations.
3. **Vault-bound Relics**
   - Accessible via vault outcomes.
4. **Path-locked Relics**
   - Require inferred alignment/path resonance.

## Consequences & World Response
Relic use can:
- Attract factions or specific NPC archetypes.
- Change future mission offerings.
- Trigger world events or escalation.
- Modify endings/epilogue outcomes.
- Create subtle gameplay advantages for true-site discoverers (e.g., faster activation, hidden dialogue, minor buffs when revisiting).

## Discovery Rule
Relics and relic sites must be discoverable organically:
- faint pulses, glow, silhouettes
- echo hallucinations near sites
- environmental storytelling (bones, collapsed camps, ghost flickers)
- subtle terrain shaping that “pulls” attention without explicit UI labels
