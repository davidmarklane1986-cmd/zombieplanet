# Combat & Wildlife

## Combat (Demo-to-Alpha Philosophy)
Combat supports tension and consequence, not constant grind.

### Player combat scope
- Bare hands by default
- Improvised weapons (sticks, stones) with low damage
- Knife/melee unlocks and upgrades later
- Firearms later (post-initial demo scope)

### Combat rules
- Not every encounter must be winnable.
- Options to fight, flee, negotiate, or align with hostile groups where appropriate.

## Wildlife
- Predators and prey
- Roaming + reactive behavior (sound/movement)
- Populates uninhabited worlds
- Supports hunting/gathering mission types
