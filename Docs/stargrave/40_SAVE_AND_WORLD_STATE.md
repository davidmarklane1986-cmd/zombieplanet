# Save & World State

## What must be tracked
- Player alignment drift (inferred, not shown as a meter)
- True relic sites discovered vs shadow copies interacted with
- Vault visits and outcomes
- Faction standing/influence changes
- Planet/moon visitation and significant discoveries
- Settlement states (growth/decay)

## What world state affects
- NPC dialogue branches and availability
- Mission offerings and difficulty
- Faction hostility and patrol presence
- Settlement expansion/ruin
- Relic behavior and consequences
- Epilogue/library “book” content and achievements

## True vs Shadow Recognition
The system must distinguish:
- Players who found canonical locations (true sites)
- Players who only saw shadow copies
This difference should influence:
- Achievements/epilogue events
- Minor gameplay advantage in MMO (e.g., faster activation at true sites, hidden dialogue)
