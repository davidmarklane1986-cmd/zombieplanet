# Systems Core (Non-Negotiables)

## Game Thesis
Echoes of the Stargrave is a world where:
- The universe exists independently of the player.
- NPCs *arrive* instead of waiting.
- History is written by systems, not cutscenes.
- Relics change reality and relationships, not just stats.
- The “ending” is the player’s story being archived.

## What the game *is*
- A persistent exploration RPG with emergent narrative.
- A single shared universe with fixed stars/planets.
- A system-driven mission and faction ecosystem.
- A relic/vault/path framework that shapes outcomes.

## What the game *is not*
- Not a hub-based quest MMO.
- Not an instanced theme-park.
- Not a tutorial-led experience.
- Not a stat-only loot grinder (relics are different).

## Non-negotiable rules
- **One universe** (single shard): no instancing, no “your private copy of the world.”
- **No loading screens between stars** (warp is allowed; teleport is not).
- **No forced tutorials** (discoverable systems).
- **No quest hubs** (NPC emergence + PME drives content).
- **No explicit morality meters** (alignment inferred).
- **Relics are narrative operators** (world reacts).
- **Build World Once** (world layout persists).
