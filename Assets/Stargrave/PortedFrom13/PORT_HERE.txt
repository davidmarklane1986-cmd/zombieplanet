Stargrave 1.3 — copy these into THIS folder (keep names or update StargravePort13SkyAtmosphereMenu.cs paths)

SKYBOX (URP)
  Copy your 1.3 skybox Material (.mat) and any Textures / Cubemaps it references.
  Preferred name: Skybox.mat
  Shader is usually: Universal Render Pipeline > Skybox > Panoramic (or 6 Sided)

ATMOSPHERE (planet rim)
  If 1.3 used a custom atmosphere shader + material, copy the .shader(s) and .mat.
  Preferred name: Atmosphere.mat
  Then assign that material on ColourSettings (atmosphere material) or run:
  Tools > Stargrave > Apply Ported 1.3 Sky (PortedFrom13)

DAY / NIGHT (optional)
  Add PlanetDayNightController to the scene; after applying the port menu, it will use the
  panoramic sky material for tint/exposure day-night if configured.

After copying, use menu:
  Tools > Stargrave > Apply Ported 1.3 Sky (PortedFrom13)
